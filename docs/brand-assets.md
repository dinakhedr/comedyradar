# Comedy Radar — Brand Assets

Brand reference for building the website. Full source: *Comedy Radar — Brand Guidelines* (claude.ai artifact, owned by Dina) — export the logo and icon SVG files from it into `public/brand/`.

## Platform
- **Name:** Comedy Radar · **Tagline:** If it's live and it's funny, it's on the radar.
- **Coverage zones:** Local Range (shows in the country), Outbound Range (its comedians on tour), Inbound Range (visiting comedians). Launch market: Egypt.
- **Radar vocabulary:** On the Radar (upcoming), New Signal (new show), Strong Signal (trending), Fading Signal (last tickets), Track (follow), Within Range (near you), New Territory (new country), Spotlight (featured carousel).
- **Contact:** comedyradarnetwork@gmail.com · Instagram **@comedy_radar** (only social account). English only at launch. Domain on hold.

## Logo
- Wordmark: **COMEDY** stacked above **RADAR**, Luckiest Guy, uppercase.
- Dark version (primary): white COMEDY, yellow RADAR on Radar Purple.
- Light version: purple COMEDY, deep pink (#E63E7A) RADAR on white.
- Icon: retro stage microphone with "volume" waves on both sides growing longer outward.
- Icon alone → inside a Spotlight Yellow rounded square (corner 24%), purple mic, purple / pink / purple waves (app icon, favicon, Instagram profile picture).
- Icon with the wordmark → no square. Waves on dark: yellow, pink, pink 55%. On light: pink, purple, yellow.

## Typography
- Display: **Luckiest Guy** — uppercase, short headlines only.
- Text: **Nunito** 400 / 700 / 800.
- Scale (px): display-xl 64 · display-lg 48 · display-md 32 · heading 22 · body 16 · small 14 · label 13.

## Shapes and patterns
- Radius: cards 20px (Spotlight 28px), buttons 12px, chips fully rounded.
- Public site: purple base, Radar Plum cards, yellow primary buttons with purple text, pill filter chips, yellow newsletter band.
- Creator dashboard: lavender background, white cards, purple sidebar with yellow active item.
- Admin panel: Stage Black sidebar, pink "SUPER ADMIN" label, yellow active item, white "ADMIN PANEL" top bar.
- Primary CTA "Get tickets"; tour cards "View tour"; sold out "Join waitlist". No prices on cards (show page only).
- Social posts: purple background with faint radar waves, logo top left, one big headline with one yellow line, photo in a rounded frame, one yellow call to action.
